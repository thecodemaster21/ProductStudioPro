# Feature tour

![Product Studio Pro feature highlights](images/main-preview.png)

## 01 · Build your studio

Five presets cover automotive, watch, perfume, headphone and packaging scenes. The automotive rig uses the complete selected product collection's bounds and replaces its own tagged rig when rebuilt. Add softbox, strip, rim and three-point lighting, then choose a black or gray backdrop or a shadow catcher.

## 02 · Shape the reflections

Rectangle, strip and edge reflection cards help control highlights on reflective surfaces. Duplicate or mirror cards, adjust their dimensions and transforms, and tune strength, gradient and color. Use RGB colors or Kelvin temperature and tint. A separate visibility control governs whether the card is visible in the render.

## 03 · Frame the shot

Hero, Macro, Beauty 45° and Side Profile provide starting camera positions. Match the current viewport, look through the scene camera, adjust focal length and enable depth of field. Presets are starting points: inspect the framing for each product.

## 04 · Render the deliverables

Set up Cycles, select a quality preset and choose beauty, clay, wireframe or transparent output. Render one shot or all scene cameras. The production workflow organizes output beside the saved Blender file, optionally into camera folders, and avoids overwriting existing filenames by adding a suffix.

## Materials and preparation

- **THAR ROXX:** a library of 25 materials; apply to the active slot or selected faces in Edit Mode.
- **Headphones:** an 11-material library with variants and name-based assignment.
- **Imports:** FBX, the bundled 3ds Max importer and PBR material import.
- **Repair:** material validation and recursive texture relinking.
- **Selection tools:** isolate, hide and restore visibility.
- **Vehicle parts:** join and rename, assign a shared material, group under a parent, center pivots and report FBX preparation issues.

The FBX check is diagnostic. It does not perform export or guarantee compatibility with an Unreal project.
